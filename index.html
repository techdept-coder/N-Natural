Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a license to work as a natural hair stylist here?
It depends on the location. For our DMV roles in Maryland, no license is required to apply, and our entry-level stylist role includes paid training. For Brooklyn, NY positions, New York requires a valid natural hair license or cosmetology license. If you are early in your career and based in the DMV, the entry-level stylist role is a strong place to start.
What does the pay look like?
Pay varies by role and experience. Entry-level stylists typically earn hourly plus tips. Experienced stylists work on a commission or salary-tiered model. Specific ranges are shared during the interview process so you know exactly what to expect.
Can I work part-time?
Some roles, like Executive Assistant, are part-time. Most stylist positions are full-time so you can build a consistent book and grow with the team. Flexible scheduling is part of how we support real life.
I am new to natural hair. Can I still apply?
Yes—especially for our Entry-Level Stylist role. We provide paid training in our clinical texture methods. What matters most is passion for natural hair, coachability, and commitment to growing with the team.
What kind of support and training will I get?
You get paid onboarding training, weekly and monthly skill sessions, and our team stylist summit. HR, marketing, and booking are handled for you so you can focus on clients and craft.
Where are your locations?
We are hiring now in Silver Spring, MD and Brooklyn, NY. Role availability can differ by location—check each job card for details.
How do I apply?
Pick a role from Open Roles, click Apply, and submit the form with your portfolio or Instagram. If there’s a match, we’ll invite you to a technical interview and salon tour.
